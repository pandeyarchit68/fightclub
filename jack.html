JACK MODE

The quiet life of a corporate ghost.
Sleepless nights. Endless routines.
A life built on catalogs and coffee.

The Routine

  • Morning

    Wake up tired. Coffee machine humming.
    Another day begins before the last one even ended.

  • Work

    PowerPoint slides. Meetings that could have been emails.
    The illusion of productivity.

  • Night

    Television flickers in a silent apartment.
    Sleep refuses to come.

The IKEA Nesting Instinct

What kind of dining set defines me as a person?
The perfect lamp. The perfect couch.
A life assembled piece by piece like flat-pack furniture.

We buy things we don't need to impress people we don't like.

Insomnia

When you have insomnia, you're never really asleep.
And you're never really awake.

Reality begins to feel like a copy of a copy of a copy.

Support Groups

  • Testicular Cancer

    Jack finds peace in rooms full of strangers sharing pain.

  • Parasites Anonymous

    Pretending to suffer finally allows him to feel something real.

  • Emotional Release

    In the arms of strangers, Jack finally sleeps.

Then He Met Tyler

Without rules.
Without the illusion of control.
